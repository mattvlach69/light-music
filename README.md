# Music (Light Phone III tool)

Browse by Artists, Albums, Playlists and Songs. Tap a song to play from there, or PLAY / SHUFFLE a whole list.
Music keeps playing after you leave the tool.

Adding music: open Tool Manager on your computer, choose Music, then Songs. Upload audio files
(MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WAV). Tags give artist and album; untagged files can sit in Artist/Album folders.

Playlists: make them on the phone (Playlists > New playlist, then + on Now playing to add songs),
or upload .m3u files to Playlists in Tool Manager. One song path per line, relative to Songs.
